# ReVoice Privacy

ReVoice is designed to minimize data collection.

- Communication history, favorites, and care-profile details are stored locally on the device.
- No account is required for the MVP.
- Intent fragments and selected photos are sent to the configured ReVoice backend only when the user requests AI suggestions.
- The backend forwards that request to OpenAI and does not intentionally persist photos or communication content.
- Subscription status is handled by RevenueCat when configured.

Do not use ReVoice for emergencies. ReVoice is an assistive communication tool, not a medical device or medical service.
